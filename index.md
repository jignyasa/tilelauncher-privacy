# Tile Launcher – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_ · Developer: JYS Studio · Contact: [jignyasatandel2380@gmail.com](mailto:jignyasatandel2380@gmail.com)

Tile Launcher is a home-screen app. It has no account system and runs no servers.
Almost everything it reads stays on your phone. This policy explains what it uses,
why, and the two cases where data leaves the device: the weather lookup and ads.

## Data that stays on your phone

Each feature below only works after you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time
in Android Settings. Nothing in this list is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

| Feature | Permission | What is read |
|---|---|---|
| App grid, drawer, search | Package visibility | The list of installed apps, so they can be shown |
| Contacts in search | Contacts | Contact names that match what you type in search |
| Today: Calendar card | Calendar | Today's event titles and times |
| Photo tile and gallery | Photos | Your latest photo and albums, shown on the tile |
| Steps tile | Physical activity | The phone's step counter |
| Screen Time card | Usage access | Today's total time spent in apps |
| Notification badges | Notification access | How many notifications each app has. Notification text is never read. |
| Double-tap to lock | Accessibility service | Nothing. The service only performs "lock screen" and cannot see the screen or what you type. |

Your layout and settings are stored on the phone. Android's own backup can copy your
settings, but not activity data such as notification counts or step counts.
"Back up layout" writes a file to a location you choose. We never receive that file.

## Data that leaves your phone

**Weather.** If you allow approximate location, the phone sends a location rounded to
about 1 km to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (api.open-meteo.com) to get the
local forecast. The request contains no identifiers. Android's geocoder, which may be
provided by Google, turns the location into a city name.

**Ads.** The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's
advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location it implies), and how you
interact with ads, to serve and measure ads. In regions that require consent (EEA, UK,
Switzerland) you are asked first, and you can change your choice later in Settings.
See [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

We do not sell your data, and we do not collect analytics of our own.

## Children

Tile Launcher is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Because we hold no
data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. For ad data, reset your
advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
